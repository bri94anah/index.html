# Beginner Planet Fitness Machine Routine

A simple, machine-only workout routine designed for 2 days a week, alternating your schedule every other week. 

---

## 📅 Schedule Rotation

This routine alternates between two different 2-day weekly schedules every other week:

* **Week A (Odd Weeks):** Tuesday & Wednesday Nights
* **Week B (Even Weeks):** Wednesday & Thursday Nights

---

## 🦾 Week A: Arms, Back, and Abs
*Used during Week A (Tuesday & Wednesday nights).*

Perform both workout days during this week. All exercises utilize Planet Fitness selectorized machines or cables (no free weights, no bodyweight movements, and no leg raises).

### Day 1 & Day 2 Routine
1. **Lat Pulldown Machine** (Back)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 10–12 reps
2. **Seated Cable Row Machine** (Back)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 10–12 reps
3. **Machine Biceps Curl** (Arms)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 10–12 reps
4. **Cable Triceps Pressdown** (Arms - using straight bar or rope attachment)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 10–12 reps
5. **Abdominal Crunch Machine** (Abs)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 12–15 reps
6. **Ab Coaster** (Abs)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 12–15 reps

---

## 🦵 Week B: Legs, Arms, and Chest
*Used during Week B (Wednesday & Thursday nights).*

Perform both workout days during this week, focusing on lower body, chest, and arms using only machines.

### Day 1 & Day 2 Routine
1. **Leg Press Machine** (Legs)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 10–12 reps
2. **Seated Leg Extension Machine** (Legs)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 12–15 reps
3. **Seated Leg Curl Machine** (Legs)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 12–15 reps
4. **Chest Press Machine** (Chest)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 10–12 reps
5. **Pec Fly / Rear Delt Machine** (Chest focus)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 12–15 reps
6. **Machine Biceps Curl** (Arms)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 10–12 reps
7. **Cable Triceps Pressdown** (Arms)
   * *Sets/Reps:* 3 sets x 10–12 reps

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for Beginners
* **Rest Periods:** Rest 60 to 90 seconds between each machine set.
* **Weight Selection:** Choose a weight where the last 2–3 reps of each set feel challenging but allow you to maintain proper form.
* **Progressive Overload:** Once you can easily complete the maximum reps for all sets, slightly increase the weight on the machine during your next session.
